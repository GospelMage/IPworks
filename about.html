National Mission

Empowering Botswana Through Sovereign Digital Infrastructure

IP Botswana Vanguard was founded with a single objective: to make decentralized blockchain technology accessible, sustainable, and economically empowering for every citizen across the Republic of Botswana.

Our Philosophy

From Mining Monopolies to Human Trust Circles

Traditional cryptocurrencies rely on multi-megawatt server farms that consume scarce electrical power and centralize control among corporate mining pools abroad.

Vanguard reimagines consensus. Using the Stellar Consensus Protocol (SCP) adapted for our national community, we convert social trust and verified identity into mathematical security. Your daily energy consumption is only 0.03 kWh—less than a nightlight.
